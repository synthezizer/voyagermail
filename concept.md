# MAIN CONCEPT: Voyager

## The Gimmick:
- When you create your account, you get assinged a ring from 1-50. 
- When you create a message and send it, it "beams" the message out to all rings. The closest rings will recieve the messasage first, but it can take as long as 1 day for your message to reach all rings. 
- You can view other people's messages that appear as little rocket ships that are on their journey to the other rings, but until it has reached your ring, you may not view the message's content.
